## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: a single square iPad Home Screen app icon for a Thai Phrommayan tarot application. Use the attached card as the visual reference for Thao Maha Phrom (Brahma). Create a NEW refined, respectful Thai sacred-art illustration of this same deity, not a photo of a card: one calm frontal face and the two side faces clearly visible, ornate tall gold Thai crown, pale ivory skin, gold jewelry and touches of ruby, hints of the six arms and sacred objects near the shoulders. Frame closely around the crowned heads and upper torso so the faces and crown stay legible at 60px; simplify tiny ornament. Symmetrical centered composition, a luminous restrained gold halo behind the heads, rich deep turquoise/teal background inspired by the reference. Sophisticated clean painted illustration with crisp elegant contours, restrained gold shading, dignified expression, excellent small-icon readability. Full-bleed opaque 1:1 square, no rounded corners drawn into the image; iPad applies its own corner mask. Keep crown and faces inside the central 80 percent safe area. No letters, words, numbers, watermark, signature, card border, white margins, extra heads, extra faces, or mockup. Deliver only the finished icon artwork.
